CNC Engraver Upgrade

A GRBL-based 3018 CNC engraver upgraded with machine protection, probing and a more capable limit-switch setup.

01 — OVERVIEW

What it is

The project focuses on turning a small desktop CNC into a more reliable machine for practical prototyping and manufacturing. The work spans CAD/CAM, wiring, firmware parameters, probing and mechanical installation.

02 — SYSTEM

Technical details

MachineTTC3018 CNC engraver with approximately 300 × 180 mm working area and 43 mm Z travel.
ControllerMKS DLC32 V2.1-003 running GRBL 1.1h.
Limit switchesX, Y and Z switches installed on both sides of the axes.
ProbeZ touch-probe integrated and tested with Candle.
CAMSolidWorks DXF exports processed through ESTLCam.
MaterialsMDF, balsa and carbon-fiber sheet prototyping.
03 — ENGINEERING

What I worked on

The upgrade involved understanding GRBL alarms, probe logic, homing behavior, controller inputs and machine-coordinate workflows. The goal is a safer and more repeatable machine rather than simply adding switches.
