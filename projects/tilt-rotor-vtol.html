01 | OVERVIEW

What it is

Fixed-wing tilt-rotor VTOL UAV concept developed around a 4 kg MTOW mission, including aerodynamic, structural and flight-system design.

02 | SYSTEM

Technical details

MissionMTOW 4 kg; stall 55 km/h; efficient cruise 80 km/h; maximum cruise 120 km/h.
Wing0.2405 m² area, 1.300 m span, AR 7.027, tapered planform, NACA 2415.
PropulsionT-Motor MN3110 780KV, 11×7 in propellers, 2 × 6S batteries.
Flight electronicsPixhawk Cube Black, Here 3+ GPS, LiDAR and optical flow.
CADSolidWorks design and DXF rib manufacturing workflow.
03 | ENGINEERING

What I worked on

The NACA 2415 wing is part of this VTOL project, not a separate project. Wing, ribs, spars and propulsion integration are treated as one aircraft design problem.
