AEROSPACE / MECHANICAL DESIGN

Tilt-Rotor VTOL UAV

A fixed-wing tilt-rotor VTOL UAV concept developed around a 4 kg MTOW mission, combining aerodynamic design, mechanical architecture and flight-system integration.

SolidWorksNACA 2415VTOLPixhawk Cube Black6S LiPoCF Structures

01 — OVERVIEW

What it is

The project explores a fixed-wing tilt-rotor architecture capable of vertical takeoff/landing and efficient forward flight. The design process includes wing geometry, propulsion layout, structural concepts, flight electronics and manufacturing considerations.

02 — SYSTEM

Technical details

MissionMTOW 4 kg; stall speed 55 km/h; efficient cruise 80 km/h; maximum cruise 120 km/h.
Wing0.2405 m² area, 1.300 m span, AR 7.027, tapered planform, NACA 2415.
PropulsionT-Motor MN3110 780KV motors with 11×7 in propellers and 2 × 6S batteries.
StructureCarbon-fiber tube spars and motor mounting with 3 mm stainless rod and bearings.
Flight electronicsPixhawk Cube Black, Here 3+ GPS, LiDAR and optical-flow sensing.
CADSolidWorks design and DXF-based rib manufacturing workflow.
03 — ENGINEERING

What I worked on

The NACA 2415 wing design is part of this VTOL project, not a separate project. Wing geometry, ribs, spars and propulsion integration are treated as one aircraft design problem.
