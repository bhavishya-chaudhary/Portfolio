Magnetic Coupled Bevel Gear System

A mechanical transmission concept combining bevel gears with magnetic coupling to explore contactless power transfer and compact mechanism design.

SolidWorksBevel GearsMagnetic CouplingMechanisms3D Printing

01 — OVERVIEW

What it is

This project investigates how conventional mechanical transmission and magnetic coupling can be combined into a compact mechanism. The design work focuses on geometry, packaging, alignment and manufacturability.

02 — SYSTEM

Technical details

DesignBevel-gear transmission concept with magnetic coupling.
CADParametric mechanical design and assembly development in SolidWorks.
Fabrication3D-printing considered for rapid mechanism prototyping.
FocusPackaging, alignment, mechanical interfaces and motion transfer.
03 — ENGINEERING

What I worked on

The project is representative of the kind of mechanism-design work I enjoy: taking a physical motion-transfer problem and iterating between CAD, fabrication constraints and mechanical behavior.
